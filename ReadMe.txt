https://stronkie.itch.io/

THE GAME
Stronkie the caveman has lost the keys of the stone age! Guide him
through 8 scrolling levels and collect every key before the time runs
out, deep in the jungle - over fallen tree trunks, up vines, ropes
and chains. Armed with his trusty yo-yo he clears the Scrubblies, Hooters
and Poglets out of his way and pops the rising balloons and falling
daggers.

- Jump along the tree trunks and climb the vines (ropes and chains
  in later levels).
- Step off a ledge and Stronkie slides down diagonally. A drop of
  one floor is fine - a longer drop is fatal. So is falling into a
  pit.
- Light bulbs give 15 seconds and the light back, gems give points.
- When the timer hits zero the lights go out: in the dark the yo-yo
  goes right through the balloons and daggers. Find a light bulb!
- After the 8 levels the game loops with a twist: the stone age
  turned upside down, turned 90 degrees, then black and white with
  flashes - and the monsters get faster every loop.

CONTROLS (joystick in port 2, or the keyboard)
  left / right       walk (cursor keys)
  up                 jump (up + left/right = jump sideways),
                     on a ladder, rope or chain: climb
  down               climb down
  fire               throw the yo-yo (space)
  left/right on a ladder at a floor: step off
  M music on/off   P pause   Esc end the game / quit (on the title)
  F10 quit

POINTS
  key 100   gem 250   light bulb 100   balloon / dagger 50
  Scrubbly 200   Hooter 300   Poglet 150
  time bonus 10 per second left, 5000 for every loop
  extra life at 20000 and every 30000 after that

REQUIREMENTS
  Amiga 500, 68000 CPU, Kickstart / Workbench 3.1 (1.3 works too),
  1 MB chip RAM (8372A Agnus) - the game itself needs about 300 KB.
  Runs from the floppy, from hard disk (Install on the disk, or the
  hard drive zip) and with WHDLoad (the WHDLoad zip, F10 quits).

DEVELOPER MODE
  CTRL + ALT + D on the title. Left/right picks the start level,
  up/down the view (normal, flipped, turned, black and white).
  In the game: N = level done, I = invulnerable, T = 3 seconds left.
  No lives are lost and no high scores are saved in developer mode.
